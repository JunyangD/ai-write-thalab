# The Relationship Between Thalassemia and Ferroaging: A Bibliometric Perspective

It is worth noting that ferroptosis plays an important role in the pathophysiology of thalassemia. Moreover, iron overload significantly aggravates oxidative stress and consequently accelerates cellular senescence. Therefore, understanding the interplay between iron metabolism and aging is crucial. In conclusion, this comprehensive analysis highlights the growing importance of ferroaging in hematological research.

We screened 2,770 records and kept 39 that actually mentioned senescence. That number felt small. Then again, most groups still chase transfusion and chelation, and the handful doing real mechanistic work rarely pick thalassemia as a model. The literature is thin precisely where it should be crowded.
