It is worth noting that ferroptosis plays a crucial role in the pathophysiology of thalassemia. Moreover, iron overload significantly aggravates oxidative stress and consequently accelerates cellular senescence. Therefore, understanding the interplay between iron metabolism and aging is of great importance. In recent years, a growing body of literature has shed light on this emerging field, and it is widely acknowledged that the role of ACSL4 cannot be ignored.

This study aims to comprehensively analyze the association between thalassemia and ferroaging. In conclusion, this integrative analysis provides a solid foundation for future clinical practice and highlights the urgent need for strengthened international collaboration. Taken together, these findings underscore the importance of further mechanistic investigation.
